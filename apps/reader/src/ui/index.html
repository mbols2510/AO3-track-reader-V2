    Sync

    Signed in as

    Your TrackSync server from docker-compose. Without one, your progress is kept on this PC only.

    Tracker service optional

    When set, your tracker container checks the reading list for updates and paces all AO3 requests in one place; the reader just shows its results. Leave empty to have the reader check AO3 itself (also rate-limited).

    Your tracker checks the reading list for updates on its own schedule, even while the reader is closed (REFRESH_INTERVAL_HOURS in its .env, every 6 hours by default). Press ⟳ in the reading list to check right now.

    Bring over data from the original app

    Copies your positions, reading list and filters from the original Archive Track Reader cloud service. Nothing there is changed or deleted, and you can run it again safely.

    Advanced

    Old service offline? Upload the old app's Ao3TrackSQLite.db3 in your TrackSync dashboard instead.

    Appearance

    Whole page enlarges titles, tags, summaries and menus along with the story, like the original app.
